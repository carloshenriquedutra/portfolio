ENGINEERING NOTES

Models, boundaries & reliability

MadeiraMadeira · Lumi · People AI assistant

Make retrieval an explicit input to generation

Business problem: Employees needed timely, consistent answers to People policies without relying on a separate manual search for every question.

Technical constraint: People policies change more often than model weights; static prompts become stale and fine-tuning couples updates to training.

Design alternatives: Embed policy content in prompts or model weights, or retrieve relevant passages at question time.

Decision: Retrieve ranked knowledge chunks from a vector index and pass them as context to the language model, keeping knowledge updates independent of model deployment.

Engineering trade-off: Retrieval adds components, and response quality remains bounded by corpus freshness, coverage, and ranking quality.

Revisit when: Evaluation shows poor recall, stale sources, or recurring knowledge gaps.

MadeiraMadeira · People Analytics data platform

Materialize for access patterns; partition on event time

Business problem: People Analytics consumers needed responsive queries over growing facts while keeping operational cost and data freshness manageable.

Technical constraint: Repeated full scans of high-volume facts incur avoidable work, while dimensions have a different refresh and read pattern.

Design alternatives: Use views for every model, materialize every model arbitrarily, or choose storage by model role and query shape.

Decision: Materialize dimensions as tables and high-volume facts as tables partitioned by event date; cluster only on recurring filter or join keys.

Engineering trade-off: Materialization costs storage and rebuild work but lets recurring queries prune irrelevant partitions.

Revisit when: Scan cost, freshness requirements, or consumer filter patterns change.

MadeiraMadeira · People Analytics data platform

Separate source conformance from business semantics

Business problem: People Analytics needed dependable, reusable data products built from fragmented source systems, from raw records through business-ready Gold models.

Technical constraint: Source schemas differ in types, naming, and history; mixing cleanup with business rules makes each consumer reimplement them.

Design alternatives: Expose source-shaped tables directly to BI, or separate source normalization from analytical modeling.

Decision: Use Bronze to retain source shape, Silver to cast, deduplicate, and normalize, and Gold to publish facts and dimensions in business terms.

Engineering trade-off: An extra transformation boundary increases model count but gives each model a narrower, testable responsibility.

Revisit when: Source contracts change or a workload needs source-level detail.

Gobrax · HubSpot CRM modeling in BigQuery

Declare grain before joining entities

Business problem: Commercial teams needed a consistent way to analyze HubSpot deals and engagements across linked, mutable CRM entities.

Technical constraint: A CRM deal can link to multiple contacts, products, and activities; unrestricted joins can fan out rows and inflate measures.

Design alternatives: Aggregate source rows directly, or define each fact’s grain and key and model many-to-many associations before aggregation.

Decision: I declare grain and business key per model, represent many-to-many relations through bridge models, and apply deterministic deduplication to mutable snapshots.

Engineering trade-off: More models and assertions upstream; less silent fan-out and clearer temporal semantics downstream.

Revisit when: Source cardinality, business identity, or event lifecycle changes.

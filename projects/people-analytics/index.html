People Analytics · raw to Gold

Business problem

A new People Analytics domain needed a dependable path from fragmented source records to reusable analytical models.

My contribution

I designed the cloud foundations and delivery flow, separating ingestion, source conformance, and analytical modeling. The path to Gold models continues to evolve.

Tools and methods

BigQuery · Dataform · Cloud Composer · Cloud Run · Terraform

Engineering decisions

Materialize for access patterns; partition on event time

Technical constraint: Consumers needed responsive queries with manageable cost and freshness. Repeated full scans of growing facts incur avoidable work; dimensions have different refresh and read patterns.

Design alternatives: Use views for every model, materialize every model arbitrarily, or choose storage by model role and query shape.

Decision: Materialize dimensions as tables and high-volume facts as tables partitioned by event date; cluster only on recurring filter or join keys.

Engineering trade-off: Materialization costs storage and rebuild work but lets recurring queries prune irrelevant partitions.

Revisit when: Scan cost, freshness requirements, or consumer filter patterns change.

Separate source conformance from business semantics

Technical constraint: Source schemas differ in types, naming, and history; mixing cleanup with business rules makes each consumer reimplement them.

Design alternatives: Expose source-shaped tables directly to BI, or separate source normalization from analytical modeling.

Decision: Use Bronze to retain source shape, Silver to cast, deduplicate, and normalize, and Gold to publish facts and dimensions in business terms.

Engineering trade-off: An extra transformation boundary increases model count but gives each model a narrower, testable responsibility.

Revisit when: Source contracts change or a workload needs source-level detail.

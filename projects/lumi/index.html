Lumi · People AI assistant

Business problem

Employees need consistent, current answers about People policies and processes in Google Chat.

My contribution

I designed and built the assistant service, connecting employee questions to maintained People knowledge.

Tools and methods

Python · RAG · Google Chat · Gemini

Engineering decisions

Make retrieval an explicit input to generation

Technical constraint: A language model's parametric knowledge does not identify which approved source supports an answer to a policy question.

Design alternatives: Provide policy text in a fixed prompt, or retrieve relevant passages from a maintained knowledge base for each question.

Decision: Retrieve ranked knowledge chunks from a vector index and pass them as context to the language model, keeping knowledge updates independent of model deployment.

Engineering trade-off: Retrieval adds components, and response quality remains bounded by corpus freshness, coverage, and ranking quality.

Revisit when: Evaluation shows poor recall, stale sources, or recurring knowledge gaps.

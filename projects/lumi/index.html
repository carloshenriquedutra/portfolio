Lumi · People AI assistant

Business problem

Employees need consistent, current answers about People policies and processes in Google Chat.

My contribution

I designed and built the assistant service, connecting employee questions to maintained People knowledge.

Tools and methods

Python · RAG · Google Chat · Gemini

Engineering decisions

Look up company documents before answering

The challenge: The AI does not know the company’s current policies on its own. That content changes and needs to be maintained by the People team.

Options considered: Train the AI on the documents, include all the content in a fixed instruction, or find the passages needed for each question.

My choice: Find passages related to the question and give them to the AI to compose its answer. The knowledge base can be updated without changing the assistant’s code or training the AI again.

Benefits and limits: Answers are grounded in documents that can be checked. This requires keeping the content current and the search working; missing information or passages that cannot be found limit the answer.

When to reconsider: Search misses useful documents, sources become outdated, or recurring questions lack supporting information.
